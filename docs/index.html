Surf height is estimated from NOAA's WAVEWATCH III (GFS-Wave) forecast for open water about 30 km offshore, converted to breaking height at the beach with the Komar–Gaylord formula, Hb = 0.39 g1/5(T H02 cos α)2/5. Set waves are 1.27 × typical. The rating is a simple rule from wind and swell period. Swell heights are offshore, in feet. Heights are face height in feet and are not yet calibrated against observations.
